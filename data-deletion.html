Revoke access & request deletion

NoWayBro's public site has no user account. These steps concern the separate private tool's authorized channel data and any message you sent us.

1. Stop Google/YouTube access

If you authorized NoWayBro for your Google account, remove its access in Google Account permissions. This is the current way to revoke authorization; sending an email does not itself revoke a Google token. Revocation stops future authorized access, but it does not by itself remove videos already hosted by YouTube or necessarily remove NoWayBro's existing local records.

2. Send a deletion request

Email jerkinsailesh@gmail.com with the subject “NoWayBro data deletion.” Tell us the platform (currently YouTube), the channel name or URL, whether you want all locally held account-linked data or specific records removed, and a reply address. You may also request deletion of a contact email you sent us. Do not send passwords, OAuth tokens, API keys, or identity documents.

3. Manual request handling

Pujari Sailesh verifies control of the affected channel before acting. The operator reviews a local deletion preview, checks for active production, backup copies and related records, then removes the authorized local data and confirms the result. You can revoke OAuth access through Google Account permissions; emailing us does not automatically revoke a token. The operator will review the related n8n credential separately without asking you to disclose it. We aim to handle verified YouTube-related deletion requests promptly and within seven calendar days. The operator checks relevant backups as part of the request and removes or redacts matching copies within the applicable request deadline; the routine 30-day backup schedule does not postpone a deletion request. An active job or uncertain upload must be reconciled first to avoid deleting the wrong record. We retain a minimal deletion record to prevent accidental repeat production; it does not include the deleted video or analytics data.

What this does not delete

Deleting NoWayBro's local data does not delete your Google account or videos and data kept by YouTube. Manage those on YouTube. GitHub Pages hosting records are handled by GitHub under its own privacy policy. TikTok and Facebook Page are not connected to NoWayBro, so there are no account tokens or API data from those platforms in the current application.

Questions

For access, correction, revocation or deletion questions, write to jerkinsailesh@gmail.com. See our Privacy Policy for the categories of data held and the current retention approach.
